Use explicit section headers. Keep production notes in Sound direction, not inside lyrical lines.

02

Describe the recording

Language → genre → mood → tempo → instruments → vocal → production. Specific traits beat a pile of genre names.

03

Start with Q8 + Full

Q8 is the recommended profile. Full planning writes melody and chords before synthesis and is the normal choice for original songs.

04

Randomize first

Use Random seed while exploring. Fix the seed only when comparing one controlled change or reproducing a take.

05

Change one axis

For revisions, alter the vocal, tempo, instrumentation, lyric, or seed deliberately. Avoid changing everything at once.

06

Keep the winner

Every WAV, exact seed, visible prompts, settings, and metadata are saved in the listening room.

G2

ONE CHECKPOINT, THREE PRECISIONS

Which model profile?

RECOMMENDED

Q8 · Balanced

The best default here: official audio.cpp testing reported the fastest long-form run, strong quality, and roughly 8.7 GB peak VRAM.

MAXIMUM PRECISION

BF16 · Full

The original-precision main weights paired with the F32 VAE. Largest and slower; use when evaluating whether precision changes a final take.

LOWEST VRAM

Q4 · Compact

Smallest profile at roughly 7.6 GB peak VRAM. Good for drafts; quantization can change length, voice, or musical detail.

These are not differently trained YuE2 models. They are precision versions of the same approximately 3.6B-parameter YuE2-3B checkpoint. The profile selector also pairs the intended VAE automatically.

G5

COPYABLE RECIPES

Prompts that stay in their lane

Sound direction formula

English, [specific genre/subgenre], [mood], [BPM or tempo], [core instruments], [vocal range/timbre/delivery], [production and mix]

Put all sonic instructions here. Avoid artist names; describe the traits you actually want.

Vocal direction

Warm low alto, close-mic and restrained in verses, clear consonants, stronger chest voice in chorus, light natural vibrato, sparse doubles, stacked chorus harmonies

Change at least range/timbre, delivery, and harmony treatment when you want a meaningfully different singer.

Lyric-writing request

Write an original [language] song about [specific situation] from [point of view]. Use concrete imagery and a concise repeated hook. Target [short/standard/long]. Return only singable lyrics using [Verse], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus], [Bridge], and [Outro]. No production notes, chords, or artist imitation.

The built-in Lyric Assistant already enforces a stricter version of this recipe.

Revision request

Keep the chorus and point of view. Rewrite verse two with more concrete imagery, tighten each line for singing, remove filler and clichés, and return the complete revised song in the same section format.

Name what must stay, what must change, and request the complete song rather than a patch.

Reliable lyric structure

[Intro]

[Verse]
Four to eight focused lines

[Pre-Chorus]
Build tension briefly

[Chorus]
Repeat the central hook

[Verse]
Develop the story

[Bridge]
Add contrast

[Chorus]
Repeat the hook

[Outro]

Keep these out of lyrics

  • BPM, key, mix, instrument, or camera instructions
  • Markdown headings and explanatory paragraphs
  • Artist names or requests to imitate a copyrighted song
  • Long parenthetical stage directions likely to be sung aloud
  • Too many unrelated styles or conflicting vocal descriptions

Planning modes

  • Full: original songs; generates melody and chords.
  • Melody: supplied melody/cover; harmony remains flexible.
  • Direct: faster experiments without a symbolic score.
G6

DIAGNOSE BY SYMPTOM

When results feel too similar

Same singer or melody

Set Seed behavior to Random. Change the visible vocal range/timbre—not just mood—and remove a supplied ABC score if you do not intend to preserve melody.

Assistant “bleedover”

There is no hidden assistant context in generation. Only clicking Use in Compose copies title, style, and lyrics. Check those visible boxes; the assistant direction is now visible and clearable too.

Muddy or confused result

Use one primary genre, one secondary influence, a coherent instrument set, one vocal description, and a single production era. Contradictions weaken control.

Words or directions get sung

Move every non-lyrical instruction to Sound direction. Keep lyrics to simple section headers and singable lines.

Need a fair comparison

Switch to Fixed seed, keep lyrics and all other settings identical, then change exactly one style phrase or model profile.

Want better odds

Generate several random seeds and keep the strongest. The official benchmark’s best-of-8 result explicitly selects among eight candidates.
